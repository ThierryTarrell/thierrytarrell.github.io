What each tab does

A quick tour of the dashboard. Tabs marked ANALYST ONLY are only visible to VTK's account — everything else here is what your login can see and use.

RoleWhat they see
Analyst (Owner)Everything — full squad management, match logging, tactics, all AI reports.
CoachOverview, Players, Match Analysis, Compare Players, Tactical Board, Fixtures, Injury Tracker, Messages, Season Report, Match Sheet, Season Sheet, Player Stats, Help & Guide.
President / Executive MemberOverview, Fixtures, Messages, Season Report, Match Sheet, Season Sheet, Player Stats, Help & Guide — a decision-focused view without squad-management or coaching tools.
PlayerOverview, Fixtures, Messages, Season Report, Season Sheet, Help & Guide, plus a personal Player Stats view — their own numbers and where they rank in the squad, not the full squad table or player comparisons.
